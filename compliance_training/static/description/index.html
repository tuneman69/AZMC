Compliance Training & Competency Management

A complete training tracking system built for quality-driven organizations

Assign, track, and verify employee training compliance — online or on-the-job — with full audit history, digital signatures, competency quizzes, and automated email notifications. Built for ISO 9001, AS9100, IATF 16949, and any regulated manufacturing or service environment.

See It In Action

Key Features

 Training Assignment & Tracking

  • Assign training to individual employees or in bulk by Job Role or Tag
  • Track status at every stage: Draft, Sent, Completed, Expired
  • Automatic expiry based on retraining frequency settings
  • Full chatter audit trail on every training record

 Online & On-The-Job Delivery

  • Online: employee gets a secure link, reviews content, passes quiz, signs digitally
  • OTJ: manager verifies practical competency and records result in Odoo
  • Supports video (YouTube embed) and file download content types

 Competency Quiz & Digital Signature

  • Build True/False quizzes on each training content record
  • Configurable minimum passing score (default 100%)
  • Employees sign directly in their browser — no Odoo login required
  • Signature image stored on the training record and printed on certificate
  • Failed attempts redirect trainee back to review materials

 Revision Control

  • Issue a new revision of any training document with one click
  • Old revision is automatically archived
  • Pending assignments on old revision are cancelled automatically
  • Wizard identifies previously trained employees for instant reassignment
  • Revision letter tracked on every log record (Rev A, Rev B, etc.)

 Automated Email Notifications

  • Training assignment emails sent via your existing Odoo mail configuration
  • Secure tokenized link in every email — no employee Odoo account needed
  • Bulk send with one click after batch assignment
  • No external mail service required

 Certificate of Competency (PDF)

  • Print a Certificate of Competency from any completed training log
  • Includes trainee name, training title, revision, completion date, and score
  • Digital signature image embedded for online completions
  • Manager verification line for OTJ completions

 Historical Training Import

  • Import existing training records from a CSV file in one step
  • Auto-creates trainees and content records if they do not exist
  • Skips duplicate records automatically
  • Detailed import summary with row-level error reporting
  • Excel-friendly UTF-8 BOM support

 Security & Access Control

  • Three-tier role system: User, Manager, Administrator
  • Users can view training records only
  • Managers can assign, verify OTJ, and manage trainees
  • Administrators have full system control
  • Public portal uses secure tokens — no employee Odoo account needed
